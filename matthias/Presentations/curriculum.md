
# How to Design a Curriculum

Designing a curriculum calls for a careful consideration of what
students entering a major know and what the consumers of a college's
"product" (aka students) would like to receive.  In turn, designing a
course in a curriculum needs to know what students bring along and
what kind of value the course adds so that downstream courses can rely
on it. 

This talk distills my experience with curriculum design from the past
three decades, starting at Rice in 1992. I will then explain how I
designed the core of Northeastern's software curriculum.  I also argue
that a software curriculum belongs into a Liberal Arts curriculum, and
that software majors would greatly benefit from a well-designed
Liberal Arts curriculum.

The presentation demonstrates how the design principles for software
actually apply to the design of a curriculum---the ultimate
meta-Liberal Art.
