19.1 Design Choices🔗

A language designer can fix this problem in a number of ways:

  1. linting, an optional pass over the program that guesses at typos

  2. explicit variable declarations at the top

  3. explicit variable declarations anywhere but hoisted to the top.

The second alternative is most commonly realized in languages and IDEs.
