Why do most languages allow programmers to declare variables close to their use?

23 The Pragmatics Question🔗

Can a single developer work in a divide-and-conquer fashion? Tackle difference pieces separately?

- yes by first writing down which part of the problem uses which variables

- but that’s bad ... a protocol that even a single person can easily violate, - not to speak of teams

23.1 Design Choices🔗

  1. a renaming tool, which consistently renames a dev’s variables other than "comm. vars"

  2. nested blocks of variable declarations; reduce "global" vars to "comm. vars"

pragmatics we want to make working in sprints (or even teams) as easy as possible from the PL side

23.2 Costs and Benefits🔗

The language designer has to modify the checks for well-formed and valid sentences

- the language designer has: (1) to modify the execution machine and (2) to add a rename-and-lift "compiler"

- the developer must understand a new concept: lexical scope

- different developers get to use the same variable w/o hesitation
