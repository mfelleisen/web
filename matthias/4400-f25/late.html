—Late Policy🔗

Wake me up

There is no credit for any late homework.

I will present the solutions to some of the assignments right after they are due. If not, you or some of your peers will do so. Hence all assignments must be turned in on time.

(The due dates and the due times are deliberately set to avoid any conflicts with holiday dates. If there are additional conflicts, send email.)
